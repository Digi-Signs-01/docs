# Schedules

**Schedules** play specific content at set dates and times — perfect for time-bound
campaigns, dayparting (different content morning vs evening), or one-off events. Outside a
schedule's window, the screen falls back to its assigned playlist.

![The Schedules page](https://digiboardimages.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/dashboard/schedules.png)

## The schedule list

Each schedule shows the **Schedule Name**, the **Scheduled Content** (and its duration),
the target **Screens** and **Groups**, the **Start time** and **End time**, and a
**Status**:

- **Scheduled** — set for a future time.
- **Executed** — its time window has passed.

## Creating a schedule

1. Click **New Schedule** (top right).
2. Name the schedule.
3. Choose the **content** to play (a playlist or media item).
4. Choose the **screens** and/or **groups** it should play on.
5. Set the **start** and **end** date/time.
6. **Save** — the schedule activates automatically at its start time.

Use **Select Status** to filter the list (for example, to see only upcoming schedules).

> [!note]
> Schedules take priority over a screen's default playlist during their window. When the
> window ends, the screen automatically returns to its regular content.
