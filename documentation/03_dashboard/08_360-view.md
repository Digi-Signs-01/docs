# 360 View

**360 View** is a single-page health report for your entire account. It pulls together
usage, content, activation, and subscription data so you can understand your deployment at
a glance.

![The 360 View overview](https://digiboardimages.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/dashboard/the-360-view.png)

## Top metrics

At the top are your organisation's headline numbers:

- **Total Screens**, **Currently Active**, **Logged In**, **Never Logged In**, and
  **Total Media**.
- A status badge showing whether the account is **active** and its plan (e.g. *trial*).

## Insight panels

- **Screen Naming Quality** — how many screens have **real names** versus **generic** ones.
  Real names indicate active, well-managed usage.
- **Monthly Activations** — a chart of how many screens were activated each month.
- **Media Breakdown** — your library by type (Video, Weblink, Image, YouTube, PDF, Audio).

![360 View — media breakdown, subscription, and activation details](https://digiboardimages.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/dashboard/the-360-view-details.png)

- **Subscription & Account** — Plan, Billing Cycle, Screen Limit, User Limit, Storage,
  Credits, Subscription End date, and Last Login.
- **Activation Details** — when screens were activated, grouped by month.
- **Team Members** — the people on your account.

> [!tip]
> 360 View is the fastest way to check your remaining limits (screens, users, storage)
> and your subscription end date before they affect your displays.
