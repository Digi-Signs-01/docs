# Dashboard (home)

The **Dashboard** is the landing page you see immediately after signing in. It gives an
at-a-glance health check of your whole account, so you can spot problems (offline or
unassigned screens) before they become an issue.

![The Digisigns dashboard home page](https://digiboardimages.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/dashboard/dashboard-home.png)

## What's on the page

- **Greeting & weather** — a personalised greeting ("Good morning, …") along with the
  current temperature and conditions for your registered location.
- **Screen status** — your total number of screens, split into four states:
    - **Playing** — actively displaying content right now.
    - **Online** — connected but not currently playing.
    - **Offline** — not reachable (powered off, no internet, or the player isn't running).
    - **Unassigned** — registered but with no content/playlist assigned yet.
- **Subscription plan** — your current plan (for example *Trial*), with your **Users**,
  **Storage**, **Screens**, and **Valid Till** date. The **Upgrade Plan** button takes you
  to plan options.
- **Storage** — total storage allowance with **Used** and **Free** amounts and a usage bar.
- **Screens location** — a world map plotting the physical location of each screen, so you
  can see your deployment geographically.
- **Screens info** — a table of your screens showing what each is **Playing**, its
  **Status**, and its **Location**, with the time it was last updated.

> [!tip]
> If a screen shows **Offline**, check the player's power and internet connection first.
> If it shows **Unassigned**, assign a playlist or schedule to it from the
> [Screens](02_screens.md) page.

> [!note]
> Trial accounts are limited (for example, 5 screens / 500 MB storage / 1 user). Use
> **Upgrade Plan** to raise these limits.
