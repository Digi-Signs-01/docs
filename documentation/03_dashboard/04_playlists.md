# Playlists

A **playlist** is an ordered sequence of media that plays as a loop. Playlists are the
most common way to put content on a screen: build the loop once, then assign it to one or
many screens.

![The Playlists page](https://digiboardimages.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/dashboard/playlists.png)

## The playlist list

Each playlist shows:

- **Playlist Name** with a summary of its contents (e.g. *2 Videos, 1 Image*).
- **Duration** — the total loop length.
- **Used By** — how many screens are currently playing it (or *Not in use*).
- **Status** — **Active** or inactive.

The three-dot menu on each row lets you edit, duplicate, rename, or delete a playlist, and
assign it to screens.

## Creating a playlist

![The playlist editor — name the playlist, then add and order media from your library](https://digiboardimages.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/dashboard/create-playlist.png)

1. Click **Create Playlist** (top right).
2. Give the playlist a name.
3. Add items from your [Media](03_media.md) library — images, videos, web links, YouTube,
   and apps.
4. **Re-order** items by dragging them into the sequence you want.
5. Set the **duration** each image/web item stays on screen (videos play their full
   length).
6. **Save**, then publish the playlist to a screen or a [group](02_screens.md#groups).

Use **New Folder** to keep large numbers of playlists organised.

> [!tip]
> One playlist can be reused across many screens. Update the playlist once and every
> screen using it picks up the change on its next content sync.
