# Media

**Media** is your content library — every image, video, PDF, web link, and YouTube clip
you've added lives here, ready to drop into a playlist or publish to a screen.

![The Media library](https://digiboardimages.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/dashboard/media-library.png)

## The media list

Each item shows:

- **Media Name** — the file name and who uploaded it.
- **Type** — **Image**, **Video**, **YouTube**, **PDF**, **Weblink**, or **Audio**.
- **Upload Date** — when it was added.
- **Properties** — resolution (e.g. *Full HD*, *1920 × 1080px*) and file size.
- **Playlists** — how many playlists currently use this item.
- **Content Moderation** — **Approved** or **Blocked** (see below).

## Uploading content

Use the toolbar at the top:

- **Upload Media** — add files from your computer (images, videos, PDFs, audio). You can
  also add a **YouTube** link or a **Weblink** to display a web page.
- **New Folder** — create folders to keep large libraries organised.
- **Search**, **Select Media Type**, and **Select Resolution** filter the library.

> [!tip]
> Match your media's resolution and orientation to your screens. Vertical (portrait)
> screens need vertical content; a 1920 × 1080 video will letterbox on a portrait display.

## Content moderation

Every item carries an **Approved** or **Blocked** status. Blocked media will not play on
screens — this lets an administrator review and gate content before it goes live. Approve
or block items from the row's actions.

> [!note]
> Storage counts against your plan's limit (shown on the [Dashboard](01_dashboard-home.md)).
> Delete unused media to free space, or upgrade your plan for more.
