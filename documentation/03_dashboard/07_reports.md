# Reports

**Reports** gives you an audit trail and analytics for your account. It expands into two
sub-pages: **User Activity** and **Analytics**.

![The User Activity report](https://digiboardimages.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/docs/dashboard/reports-user-activity.png)

## User Activity

A chronological **audit log** of everything that happens in your account — logins,
password resets, content changes, screen events, and more. Each entry shows the **user**,
the **activity**, the user's role, and a **timestamp**.

Filter the log with **Filter Activity**, **Filter Section**, **Filter User**, and
**Filter Screen**, then **Export CSV** or **Download PDF** to share or archive it.

> [!tip]
> Use User Activity to see who changed a playlist or who a screen was last edited by —
> useful when several team members share an account.

## Analytics

The **Analytics** sub-page reports on playback and engagement — for example which content
played, how often, and on which screens — so you can measure what your network is actually
showing. Combine it with [Reports → User Activity](#user-activity) for a full operational
picture.
