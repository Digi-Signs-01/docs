# Dashboard Guide

The Digisigns dashboard at **[dashboard.digisigns.in](https://dashboard.digisigns.in)** is
where you manage everything — your screens, content, playlists, schedules, layouts, team,
and account. This guide walks through every section of the dashboard, what it does, and
how to use it.

Sign in with your email and password (or **Continue with Google**). Once you're in, the
**left sidebar** is your main navigation, and the top-right corner holds **notifications**,
**settings**, and your **profile**.
