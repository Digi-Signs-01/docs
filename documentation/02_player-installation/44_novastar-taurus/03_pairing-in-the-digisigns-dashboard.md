# Pairing in the Digisigns dashboard

Once the Taurus is [bound to the Digisigns server](02_binding-to-the-digisigns-server.md),
add it to your account using the device **serial number (SN)** as the pairing code.

## Find the serial number

The SN is printed on the label on the back of the Taurus device, and is also shown next to
the player in **ViPlex Express**.

## Add the player

1. Log in to your Digisigns account and go to the **Screens** section.
2. Click **+ Add Screen**.
3. In the **Pairing / Registration Code** field, enter the Taurus **SN**.
4. Click **Save**.

The player registers with your account within a minute, and any assigned content begins
playing on the LED display.

> [!note]
> If the player doesn't appear, confirm it shows **online** in ViPlex Express and that the
> server binding in [step 2](02_binding-to-the-digisigns-server.md) completed successfully.
