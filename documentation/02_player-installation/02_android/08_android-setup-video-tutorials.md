# Android Setup Video Tutorials

A complete, watch-along walkthrough for setting up a Digisigns player on an Android
device — from a brand-new screen to content playing automatically. Follow the videos in
order, or jump to the step you need.

> [!tip]
> Pair these videos with the written [Installing Android App](01_installing-android-player-software.md)
> guide. Once the player is connected, manage it from the
> [dashboard](../../03_dashboard/02_screens.md).

## 1. First-time device setup

Power on a new device and complete the initial Android setup, including signing in with a
Google account so you can reach the Play Store.

## 2. Installing the Digisigns app from the Play Store

Install the Digisigns Player app directly from the Google Play Store. (For full control
over updates and the newest features, you can instead side-load the app from the
[Digisigns downloads page](https://digisigns.in/downloads) — see the written guide.)

<div class="video-embed">

</div>

## 3. Logging in with screen credentials

Open the app and log in with the screen's credentials (for example `digi1` and its
password) from your dashboard to connect the player to a screen.

If you can't log in with credentials, use the on-screen **registration code** instead —
add the device from the dashboard by entering the code the app displays.

Configure the device so the Digisigns app launches automatically on boot — essential for

## 6. Factory resetting the device

Need to start over or repurpose a screen? This shows how to factory reset an Android
device before setting it up again.

<div class="video-embed">
  <iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/A8Xg4uGiSBM" title="Factory Resetting an Android Device" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowfullscreen></iframe>

> [!note]
> A factory reset erases everything on the device. Only do this on a screen you intend to
> reconfigure from scratch.
